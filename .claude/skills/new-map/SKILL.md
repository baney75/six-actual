# New map (W2)

1. DEM: USGS 3DEP or Copernicus GLO-30 for a real location. Crop to 8×8 km and log source and license in ASSETS.md.
2. Roads, rail and rivers from OpenStreetMap (ODbL, credited), simplified.
3. Towns from the building-kit generator, at a density that fits the region and era.
4. Write the map data to `data/maps/<id>/`, generated by script.
5. Contact sheet: top-down render, the 4 RTS heights, deployment zones, and lines of sight from each objective. Open it.
6. AI-vs-AI soak on the map with mirrored decks. No side may win more than 60% from spawn position alone.

*The DEM importer, OSM pipeline and map schema are not built yet (ERA-03; one map for Phase 1).*
