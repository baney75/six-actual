# Assets

Procedural unit meshes are own work, generated by `scripts/build_models.py` in Blender 5.2.1 from the dimensions in `data/units.json`. They are game-scale silhouettes, not photogrammetry.

PREFIX game/assets/units/ own-work scripts/build_models.py

No audio files yet.

Allowed licenses when files arrive: CC0, CC-BY 4.0, MIT, own work, or a generated file with the tool and plan recorded here. `scripts/check_licenses.py` fails if a file under `game/assets/` or `audio/` is not named in this document.

Style board (Phase 1, Cold War first) is not started. It needs Donovan's sign-off before batch production.

## Locally generated audio (D020)

Generated on Parish1 through `~/.local/bin/parish-media`. Per-file tool, model, mode, instruct text, seed and job receipt go in `audio/manifest.json` (from the first real batch) or in the sidecar `.wav.json` the tool writes.

PREFIX audio/generated/ generated Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B (Apache-2.0) or ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT), local on Parish1, no paid service

- `audio/generated/smoke/xo_take_control.wav`: smoke test, Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign, job `20261008-144351-voice-f03841`. Not a shipping line.

PREFIX game/assets/audio/dialogue/ generated Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B VoiceDesign (Apache-2.0), local on Parish1, no paid service

- `game/assets/audio/dialogue/<era>/<id>.wav`: the radio dialogue matrix (`dialogue/lines.csv`, 117 lines: XO and 8 commanders), generated by `scripts/voice_lines.py` with the designed voices in `audio/voices.json` (D066).
- `audio/voices.json` (casting: voice description and seed per speaker) and `audio/manifest.json` (every spoken file with text, seed, prompt hash, loudness, duration and transcription score): own work, generated by `scripts/generate_dialogue.py` and `scripts/voice_lines.py`.

PREFIX audio/design/ own-work scripts/generate_sfx.py (sound design entries, BRIEF D7)

PREFIX game/assets/audio/sfx/ own-work scripts/generate_sfx.py

- `game/assets/audio/sfx/<family>/*.wav`: procedural sound effects synthesised with numpy (rifle, MG, cannon in near/mid/far, explosions small/medium/large, supersonic cracks, footsteps per surface, radio squelch tails per era, ambience beds, diesel and turbine engine loops, UI click; D068, D071). `audio/sfx_manifest.json` lists them (own work).

PREFIX game/assets/audio/music/ generated ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT), local on Parish1, no paid service

- `game/assets/audio/music/<era>_<layer>.ogg`: 9 battle music stems (calm, tension, combat per era; AUD-03, D067), generated by `scripts/generate_music.py` (prompt, seed and job receipt in each `.ogg.json`), normalised to -18 LUFS, looped at searched loop points. `audio/music_manifest.json` lists them (own work).

PREFIX game/assets/audio/briefings/ generated Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B CustomVoice (Apache-2.0), local on Parish1, no paid service

- `game/assets/audio/briefings/<era>/<operation>.wav`: the 33 campaign briefings (MODE-02, D065), generated by `scripts/generate_briefings.py` (speakers Ryan for Cold War and modern, Aiden for WW2; text, instruction, seed and job receipt in each `.wav.json`). They are loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP with ffmpeg, and transcription-checked by `scripts/qa_briefings.py` (word recall: mean 0.985, min 0.929).

## Authorship of 3D content (D021)

Every mesh, material, camo pattern and animation is Claude's own work, generated by scripts in this repo. No third-party or AI-generated meshes.
